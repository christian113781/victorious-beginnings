Pre-Marital Counseling: Six Sessions

Six sessions to help couples thrive towards a healthy and productive marriage and work through specific issues such as communication problems, finances, parenting and careers. Pre-marital counseling provides a safe space for healthy discussion to gain a deeper understanding of each other and work through these issues in the early stages of the relationship, while building a loving biblical foundation for marriage.

Book Recommendation

The Power of I AM: The Neuroscience of Positive Affirmations

Your words are powerful. Though they are invisible, words can be the driving force of your development, identity, and your creative potential. This beautiful picture book artistically communicates the power of the I AM statement. Spoken with joy, grace, and deeply rooted belief, our family shares our favorite I AM declarations with you and yours.
